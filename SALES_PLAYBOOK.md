# Pune Corporate Cars — B2B Sales Playbook & Pitch Guide

This document contains the complete sales strategy, demonstration script, objection handlers, and pricing matrix to pitch and close **Pune Corporate Cars India Pvt. Ltd.** (or any luxury/corporate fleet operator).

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Pitch Hook

* **The Trap:** Do not pitch this as a "website redesign" or "code changes".
* **The Reality:** Corporate car rental companies care about winning lucrative annual corporate contracts (IT MNCs in Hinjewadi/Magarpatta, 5-star hotel desks, corporate delegations) and eliminating manual dispatch overhead.
* **The Winning Pitch:**
  > *"Mr. Nalawade, we built a **24/7 Digital Ground Transportation Operating Platform** that positions Pune Corporate Cars as an international executive brand. It features your complete 15-vehicle fleet, 49 Pan-India cities, an Admin Command Center, and an **Executive AI Concierge** that calculates tariffs and books rides for corporate travel desks 24/7."*

## 4. Handling Client Objections

* **Objection: "We already get bookings via WhatsApp and direct calls."**
  > *"Phone calls work for single retail trips. But Fortune 500 companies and enterprise procurement desks require a formal digital portal, verified commercial fleet audit, and ISO accreditation to approve annual vendor contracts. This system is engineered to win those high-ticket contracts."*

* **Objection: "Will the AI make mistakes or give wrong prices?"**
  > *"No. The AI Concierge runs with strict tool-calling connected directly to your official company database. It only quotes your approved tariffs, vehicle limits, and verified cities."*

* **Objection: "Can we add new cars or change prices later?"**
  > *"Yes, that is why we built the dedicated `/admin` Command Center. Your team can view bookings, track leads, and manage records without touching any code."*
